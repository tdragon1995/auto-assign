# Driver shifts — dashboard extension

Mode: Operate. User: dispatch/payroll supervisor at a desk; occasional phone review.
Scope: retain the current dashboard shell and component family. No replacement
visual world, comp round, concept roll or new raster assets. DESIGN.md was absent
before this extension; PRODUCT.md is the incumbent authority.

## Direction contract

THESIS: See a driver's scheduled hours and correct them in one place, including
future dates, without opening Sheet or mistaking missing data for a day off.
OWN-WORLD: Existing white data surface, slate text/borders, indigo actions and blue
pen controls. Existing app typography and button family, Lucide line icons,
tabular local times. No decorative material or changed dashboard branding.
STORY: Choose a date, scan driver/time/source, edit at the top of this panel and
save. PT patterns have dated applicability and are expanded at the next MISA sync.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep dashboard header/nav. Inside one panel place the daily/PT
switch left, date/navigation/actions right, search and retention note next, then
one scrollable data table. The blue edit control stays next to each driver.
FORM: Incumbent operational table extension. No concept-roll seed applies to a
preserved world. Native date/time controls and a responsive inline editor.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
